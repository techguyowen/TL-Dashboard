Dashboard Help & Instructions Guide
Est. Out-of-Pocket Cost Calculation
Triangle Liquidators adds standard auction fees to your winning hammer bid:
  • 15% Buyer's Premium (BP)
  • 7.25% Sales Tax (applied to Bid + BP)
  • 3% Credit Card Processing Fee
Example: $100 Hammer Bid ➔ $115 Subtotal ➔ $8.34 Tax ➔ $3.70 CC Fee = $127.04 Final Total
Pickup Locations & SC Transfer
• 📍 Raleigh Pickup: 1101 Transport Dr, Raleigh, NC 27603. Directly inspect and pick up won lots locally after closing.
• 🚚 SC Transfer: Items transferred to the Williamston / Anderson, SC Depot for South Carolina bidders. Filter by location using the sidebar location selector.
Highlight Keywords & Single-Tag Isolation
• Left-Click Tag: Filters and focuses strictly on lots for that item alone (Single-Tag Isolation Mode).
• Click 'x' on Tag: Removes the keyword from your watchlist.
• Star Icon: Saves favorite lot items locally and syncs across devices.
Live Progressive Ingestion Engine
The dashboard runs a Puppeteer background crawler connected via Server-Sent Events (SSE). Lots stream progressive page-by-page as they are indexed, without freezing your browser or requiring manual page refreshes.
